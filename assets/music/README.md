Drop your own royalty-free music here to use it instead of the generated ambient beds.

- `assets/music/<mood>/*.mp3` is used for niches with that mood (dark, epic, uplifting, playful, tense, ambient)
- `assets/music/*.mp3` is used for any mood

Good free sources: YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music. Music is looped, faded and
automatically ducked under the voice.
